  <h2>English</h2>

  <p>Sachie Kobayashi (1990) is interested in intuitive creation and musical expression related to social phenomena.</p>

  <p>Born in Kanagawa, Japan, she began studying piano at seven and composition at twenty.</p>

  <p>She has received scholarships and grants including the Swiss Confederation Excellence Scholarship, the fellowship by the Program of Overseas Study for Upcoming Artists (Agency for Cultural Affairs, Japan), Nomura Foundation, and Kakehashi Foundation. After moving to Europe, her works were presented worldwide and she participated in numerous festivals. She has worked with ensembles such as Klangforum Wien, Ensemble Modern, Ensemble Reconcil, Ensemble Proton Bern, mdi Ensemble, percussion CYTi, Orchestre de la HEM, Ensemble 21, and The Geidai Philharmonia Orchestra.</p>

  <p>From 2021 to 2022, she participated in the <em>Cursus de composition et d'informatique musicale</em> at IRCAM (Paris), supervised by Pierre Jodlowski. She was shortlisted for the ISCM <em>World New Music Days 2021</em> and selected for <em>Protonwerk no. 11</em> by Ensemble Proton Bern. She received a <em>Special Mention</em> at the Ars Electronica Forum Wallis Selection 2020 for her acousmatic works.</p>

  <p>She collaborated with film director Daniel Künzi on the documentary <em>pomme de discorde</em> and with multidisciplinary artist Ekaterina Kostelskaya for video art. Recently, she has been involved in stage productions integrating video and concert.</p>

  <p>In 2020, her piece <em>I haven’t known you yet</em> for orchestra and electronics premiered with the HEM Orchestra conducted by Pierre Bleuse.</p>

  <p>She started her composing career while pursuing a second master’s degree in music theory education at Haute école de musique de Genève, after completing her composition master’s degree in 2020 with Michael Jarrell, Luis Naon, and Gilbert Nouno. She holds a bachelor's degree in Cultural Creative Studies from Aoyama Gakuin University and studied composition at Tokyo University of the Arts (Geidai) under Akio Yasuraoka. In 2018, she won the Acanthus Prize and Taito Mayor Prize.</p>

  <h2>IRCAM &amp; artistic research</h2>
  <p>During the IRCAM Cursus (2021–2022), she explored the relationship between movement, sound and video through <em>Day 0 / TRANS-INSTRUMENTALISM</em>, using the R-IoT motion sensor. The series continued with <em>RêVeRie</em> and the ongoing <em>Babel</em> installation. In 2023 she collaborated on multimedia and dance projects at Beaux-Arts de Paris; in 2024 she participated in a collaboration between Le Fresnoy and IRCAM.</p>
